Everything on the Globe key.

Use Fn (Globe) or either Option key. Nothing to open, no window to find.

  1. Hold Globe.
    Speak. Let go.

    Works anywhere you can type: a terminal, a chat box, a commit message.

  2. + ⇧

    Hold Globe and Shift.
    Let go to stop.

    The screen and the microphone are recorded while you hold, then packaged into a take folder. For a screenshot, tap Shift once instead.

  3. + ⇧2×

    Hold Globe. Double-tap Shift to lock video.

    Release both keys and keep recording your screen and narration. Tap Globe once to stop and paste.

Free. Open source. Yours to read.

MIT licence, source on GitHub. No account and no analytics. By default, your voice and your screen stay on your Mac.

  • 25 languages, on your Mac’s CPU. NVIDIA Parakeet covers English, Swedish, German, French, Spanish and 20 more.
  • Your own vocabulary. List the names it misses in vocabulary.md and it spells them your way.
  • Plain files. Takes and history live in ~/Documents/see.computer/. Read them, grep them, delete them.
  • A command line too. see-computer transcribe and see-computer clip run the same engine from a script.
  • Sharing, in beta. Turn it on and new takes upload, so you can send a link.

Four permissions, and why.

macOS asks for each one the first time a feature needs it.

Accessibility
To paste your words at the cursor.
Input Monitoring
To notice when you hold the key.
Microphone
For dictation and narration.
Screen Recording
For takes only. Dictation works without it.

Install on your Mac.

macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. The 670 MB speech model downloads on first launch.
