Dictation for the Mac. Local, free, MIT.

It writes it the way you would have typed it.

Hold a key and speak. The words land at your cursor without the um, with the numbers as figures. It is the fastest way to share what you are seeing and saying with your computer, and with the agents running on it. Hold Shift too, and the screen and your voice are captured into a folder an agent can read.

Cleanup

What happens between the model and the paste

The speech model is good. The transcript it hands back is still not what you would type. Three passes run on it, in this order, before Cmd+V.

1. Hesitation is dropped

um, uh and hm go, along with the comma or full stop stuck to them. The next word takes over the capital letter they were holding. A dictation that was nothing but a hum pastes nothing at all.

"like" and "actually" stay, because they are real words far more often than filler. "er" stays because it is Swedish for "your".

2. Numbers become figures

Parakeet on its own gets this right about half the time. So a grammar over the number words of English and Swedish runs after the filler pass and settles it.

Ten and above are digits. Zero to nine stay words unless they carry a percent sign or a decimal point, which is why "one thing" and the Swedish article "en" are left alone. Digits already in the text pass through, and the stray space the model puts in "235 ,000" is closed.

3. Your words, in vocabulary.md

Parakeet has never heard Tauri or pnpm, so it writes the nearest thing it knows. Put the words in ~/Documents/see.computer/vocabulary.md, one per line. The file is re-read on change, no restart. Case matters.

The terms are pushed into the decoder while it is still choosing, not swapped in afterwards. A find-and-replace could not tell Tauri from a "tao ry" a different sentence really did say. A term only gets its bonus when the model was already considering it, within a margin of its own best guess, so a long list stays safe. A bonus can win a close call, not overturn a confident one. Without that gate a sixty-term list turned "pnpm build" into "PNPM Buildship".

Speed, local only

155ms

A six-second Swedish sentence on an M3 Max comes back in about 155 ms after you let go of the key. The model runs on the CPU. No request leaves the machine, so there is no network round trip to wait on and nothing to send.

Recording

A take is a folder an agent can read

Hold Option and Shift and talk through what is on screen. Let go. The recording is packaged into a take, and what gets pasted is the narration in quotes, a blank line, then the names of the captures and the path to take.md. Drop that into an agent's prompt.

What lands on disk

~/Documents/see.computer/<stamp>/
├── take.md              what was said, and where to look
├── transcript.json      words with timing
├── shots/               stills taken during the take
└── clips/
    └── 001/
        ├── clip.mov
        └── frames/      JPEG frames

An agent that can read files can open the exact frames where each thing was said, without decoding the video.

The pill

A small overlay at the bottom of the screen

It tells you which of the three states you are in. If nothing could take the paste, it stretches into a card with the text and a Copy button, so a dictation is never lost to a window that had no cursor.

Listening. Cyan bars while you hold the key.

Recording. Red bars while Shift is held too.

Locked take. A lit ring until you tap again or press Esc.

Tray, history, triggers

Everything else lives in the menu bar

The tray panel lists recent dictations and recordings. Click one to copy it again. Settings hold the folder, launch at login, the history toggle, the trigger choice and shortcuts to each permission pane.

History

One markdown file per day in ~/Documents/see.computer/. Turn it off with "Save Dictation History" in Settings. Nothing is pruned automatically, so the folder grows until you delete from it.

Audio never leaves the machine. Transcript files are ordinary files, though, and if iCloud Desktop & Documents sync is on they sync with the rest of Documents. Turn history off if that matters to you.

Trigger

Choose Left Option, Right Option or Fn (Globe). On the Swedish layout Right Option is AltGr, so pick another if you type braces with it.
