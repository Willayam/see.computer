Three gestures

One key does all of it.

Left Option by default. Right Option or Fn if you prefer. Recording and locking follow whichever trigger you picked.

1

Hold, speak, release

⌥ Optionhold

Text is pasted at the cursor in any app. The audio never leaves the machine.

2

Double tap to lock

⌥⌥then tap once to finish

A locked take for long narration, so you are not holding a key for two minutes. Tap the trigger to finish and paste. Esc throws it away. Inside a locked take, hold the trigger again and use Shift for shots or clips.

3

Add Shift to record

⌥ Option+⇧ Shifthold

Records the screen and microphone while held. On release the recording is packaged into a take folder an agent can read, and a paste with the narration and the path. Let go before about 0.6 seconds and it is discarded.

The pill

Six states, one chip, no focus.

The pill is a dark overlay at the bottom of the screen. It cannot take focus, so it can never steal the keystroke that pastes. Motion is spring-driven, 0.3 s response, 0.7 damping.

Idle

Nothing to show, so it shows nothing. The pill stays out of the way until the trigger goes down.

Listening

Cyan bars, scaled to the loudest thing heard lately. A quiet room and a loud one both fill the bars, so you can always tell the mic is picking you up.

Locked

A lit ring around the chip. A live microphone nobody is touching should never look idle.

Recording

The bars turn red while Shift is held and the screen is being captured.

Warming

A breathing dot while the model downloads, loads, or warms. It reads "Downloading model 42%", "Loading model", then "Warming up".

Nothing had focus, so the text is here.

Copyesc

Card

If no text field could take the paste, the pill stretches into a card with the text, a Copy button, and a dismiss. Nothing you said is lost.

Speed and privacy

On the CPU. Off the network.

155 msSix-second Swedish sentence, M3 Max

The speech model is NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6b v3, quantised to INT8 and run through ONNX on the CPU. It downloads once on first launch, 670 MB, into ~/Library/Application Support/see.computer/models/. After that the app works with the network cable pulled out.

Parakeet v3 covers 25 European languages. The README shows English and Swedish.

Your audio is never uploaded. The one caveat is on the transcript side. If iCloud Desktop & Documents sync is on, the history files in ~/Documents/see.computer/ go wherever iCloud sends them. Turn history off in the tray if that matters to you.

Recording for agents

A take is a folder, not a video.

Hold Option and Shift, talk over what is on screen, let go. The screen and the mic are packaged into a folder with the video, the frames, and the words with timestamps. An agent that can read files opens the exact frame where you said "this one" without decoding the video. A human, or a web chatbox, still gets the words.

What gets pasted at your cursor

The paste is the narration in quotes, a blank line, then the names of the captures and the path to take.md. Paste it into an agent chat and the agent has everything it needs to go look.

Cleanup

What you meant, not every sound.

Three passes run before the paste. None of them has a settings screen.

Hesitation goes

um, uh and hm are dropped, along with the comma or full stop stuck to them, and the next word takes over the capital. "like" and "actually" stay because they are usually real words. "er" stays because it is Swedish for "your". A dictation that was only a hum pastes nothing.

  • Um, so the ring→So the ring
  • hm→nothing pasted
  • er bil→er bil

Numbers become figures

Ten and above are digits. Zero to nine stay words unless they carry a percent sign or a decimal point. Works for English and Swedish.

  • two hundred and seventy nine→279
  • twenty-five thousand→25,000
  • Fable five point one→Fable 5.1
  • tjugo procent→20 %
  • two million→2 million

Your words, in the decoder

Put words the model misses in ~/Documents/see.computer/vocabulary.md, one per line. They are pushed into the decoder while it is still choosing, not swapped in afterwards. A term only gets its bonus when the model was already considering it, so a long list stays safe. A bonus wins a close call. It does not overturn a confident one. The file is re-read on change, no restart.

The tray

Everything else lives in the menu bar.

Click the menu bar icon and you get the recent dictations and recordings. Click one to copy it again. That is most of what the panel is for.

History. One markdown file per day in ~/Documents/see.computer/. Plain files you can read, grep, or delete. Nothing is pruned for you. Turn it off with "Save Dictation History" in Settings.

Triggers. Left Option, Right Option, or Fn (Globe). Right Option is AltGr on the Swedish layout, so Swedes will probably want Left.

Settings. The folder, launch at login, the history toggle, the trigger, and shortcuts to the permission panes.

Permissions

Four prompts, and why each one.

macOS will ask. Here is what each grant is used for, so you can decide with the facts. The first two are needed for dictation at all. The last one only matters if you record.

  • Needed to paste

    Accessibility

    The app pastes by pressing Cmd+V in the frontmost app. macOS only lets a process do that with Accessibility granted.

  • Needed for hold-to-talk

    Input Monitoring

    The trigger is a bare modifier key. Watching a lone Option or Fn go down and up, in any app, needs Input Monitoring.

  • Needed to hear you

    Microphone

    The audio is transcribed on the CPU and never uploaded. Without this grant there is nothing to transcribe.

  • Only for the video hotkey

    Screen Recording

    Used only when you hold Shift with the trigger. Recording goes through the system screencapture tool. Skip this one if you only dictate.

Get it

macOS 14, Apple Silicon, and nothing else.

Download the DMG, drag it to Applications, grant the prompts, hold Option. The model downloads on first launch and the pill tells you how far along it is.
