09:12Replying to a message

Hold Option, say it, let go

Hold the Left Option key, say the reply, release. The text is pasted at the cursor, in Messages or any app with a text field. No window opens and nothing needs clicking.

The speech model is NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6b v3, an INT8 ONNX build that runs on the CPU. On an M3 Max a six-second Swedish sentence comes back in about 155 ms. The audio never leaves the machine. Nothing is uploaded, because there is no server to upload to.

The pill at the bottom of the screen shows cyan bars while it listens. They scale to the loudest thing heard lately.

10:40A long Slack post

Lock the take, talk for two minutes

A post this long is too long to hold a key for. Double tap Left Option and the take locks. Talk. Tap once more to finish and paste, or press Esc to throw the whole thing away. While a take is locked the pill wears a lit ring, so a live microphone never looks idle.

Hesitation is dropped before anything is pasted. Um, uh and hm go, along with the comma or full stop stuck to them, and the next word takes over the capital. "Like" and "actually" stay, because they are usually real words. "Er" stays too, since it is Swedish for "your". A dictation that was only a hum pastes nothing.

11:05Showing an agent a layout bug

Hold Shift too, and the agent gets the screen

The sidebar overlaps the header at narrow widths. Typing that out is slower than pointing at it. Hold Left Option and Shift together, and the screen and the microphone record while the keys are held. Let go, and the recording is packaged into a take folder.

What lands in the chat box is the narration in quotes, a blank line, then the names of the captures and the path to take.md. An agent that can read files opens the exact frames where each thing was said, without decoding the video. A human, or a web chatbox, still gets the words. Let go before about 0.6 seconds and the take is discarded, so a brushed key leaves nothing behind.

15:00Teaching it a word

The model kept missing Convex

Open ~/Documents/see.computer/vocabulary.md and add it on its own line. The file is re-read on change. No restart, no settings screen.

These words are not swapped in after the fact. They are pushed into the decoder while it is still choosing, so a term only gets its bonus when the model was already considering it. A bonus can win a close call. It cannot overturn a confident one. That is what keeps a long list safe.

17:45Reading the day back

One file per day, and nothing else

Everything dictated today is in one markdown file in ~/Documents/see.computer/. Plain text. Read it or delete it. Nothing is pruned for you, and nothing is synced by the app.

The tray panel in the menu bar lists recent dictations and recordings. Click one to copy it again. Settings live in the same panel, with the folder, launch at login, the history toggle, the trigger choice and shortcuts to each permission. Turn history off with "Save Dictation History" if you would rather keep nothing.

One caveat. The audio never leaves the machine, but the transcript files are ordinary files in Documents. If iCloud Desktop & Documents sync is on, they go wherever Documents goes.

The rest, briefly

What the day did not show. Triggers, the pill's four states, what macOS will ask you for, and how the thing is built.

The pill

A small overlay at the bottom of the screen. It only changes shape when it has something to tell you.

Triggers

Pick the trigger in the tray. Left Option, Right Option, or Fn (Globe). Right Option is AltGr on the Swedish layout. Recording and locking follow whichever trigger you picked.

  • Hold the trigger, speak, release. Pastes at the cursor.
  • Double tap, speak, tap once more. Esc discards. Inside a locked take, hold the trigger again and use Shift for shots or clips.
  • Hold trigger+Shift, release to stop. Records screen and microphone into a take.

What macOS asks for

Four permissions, each for one reason. The Settings panel has a shortcut to each.

  • Accessibility, to press Cmd+V for the paste.
  • Input Monitoring, to notice a bare modifier being held down.
  • Microphone, for the obvious reason.
  • Screen Recording, for the Option+Shift hotkey only. Skip it if you never record.

Requirements and the first launch

macOS 14 or later on Apple Silicon. The model is 670 MB and downloads on first launch into ~/Library/Application Support/see.computer/models/. It downloads once.

Takes and history live in ~/Documents/see.computer/. The bundle also carries a CLI: see-computer transcribe <wav> and see-computer clip <mov>.

How it is built

Tauri v2 and Rust. One controller thread owns the app state. The menu bar panel is native AppKit with NSGlassEffectView. No bundler, no Node toolchain. Screen recording uses the system screencapture tool.

Build from source with cd src-tauri && cargo tauri build --debug --bundles app, or take the DMG from the latest release.
